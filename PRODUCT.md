# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Approved Kallisto service providers and their authorized studio teams use Virtual Office throughout the project lifecycle. They work primarily on desktop while reviewing opportunities, requirements, deliverables, approvals, documents, commercial records, and project activity. The shell must help them move between operational tasks quickly without losing project context.

## Product Purpose

Kallisto Virtual Office is the professional workspace for service providers operating inside the Kallisto ecosystem. It gives providers one structured place to manage enquiries, projects, teams, clients, documents, calendars, and payment visibility, with Odin available as an embedded assistant. Success means the application feels immediately trustworthy, remains calm under dense project information, and keeps navigation predictable as workflows expand.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and professional. Kallisto should feel premium through restraint, alignment, and dependable interaction rather than decoration. The product voice is concise, operational, and quietly confident.

## Anti-references

- Oversized, airy SaaS dashboards with weak information density.
- Decorative gradients, excessive shadows, glass effects, and generic AI-generated card grids.
- Novel navigation patterns that slow down frequent operational work.
- Unbounded content that stretches across wide displays.
- Prototype architecture, local-only business data, or visual-only permission controls presented as production behavior.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the workspace bounded and calm while the surrounding chrome adapts.
2. Earn a premium feel through exact spacing, consistent controls, and restrained surfaces.
3. Make navigation dense, predictable, and accessible for repeat professional use.
4. Preserve project context and clear separation between core work and Odin assistance.
5. Treat responsive behavior as structural changes in the shell, not arbitrary shrinking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, accessible names, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, and controls that do not rely on color alone. Support keyboard navigation, reduced-motion preferences, desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts. Keep status and permission messaging explicit, and deny access by default when identity or authorization information is incomplete.
